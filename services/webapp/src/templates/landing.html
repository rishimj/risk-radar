How it decides

Why there's no magic threshold number.

A fixed cut doesn't work. Replaying 672 real headlines, a 0.7 threshold fired on 13.5% of windows — a single mildly negative headline scores 0.81 — while averaging sentiment instead fired on 0%. No constant sits reliably between those.

So each ticker is judged against its own trailing 24 hours. A window alerts when it lands in that ticker's top 1%. TSLA sees 11 articles an hour and AAPL 32; they get different bars, automatically.
